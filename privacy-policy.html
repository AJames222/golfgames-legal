GolfGames Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

GolfGames (the "app") is a golf scorekeeping app for the games golfers play with friends: Skins, Wolf, Bingo Bango Bongo, and tournaments. It is provided by This Is Why We Play ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. You can reach us at tiwwp222@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The app does not take bets, hold money, or process payments of any kind. Dollar amounts in the app are only calculations of who owes whom, for your group to settle among yourselves.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools, and no tracking. We do not sell your information.
  • Most of what you enter (players, courses, rounds, history) stays on your phone.
  • Some features need our servers: tournaments, live Skins rounds, the free trial, unlock codes, and emailing teams their codes. This policy lists exactly what each one sends.

Information that stays on your phone

Saved players and their handicaps, saved courses, the rounds you play in Skins, Wolf and Bingo Bango Bongo, your round history, settings, and any email addresses you type in for a tournament's players are stored on your phone. We cannot see them. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

Information sent to our servers, by feature

FeatureWhat is sent and stored
TournamentsThe tournament name, course name and hole pars, team names, player names and handicaps you enter, each team's scores, and the codes that let teams join. Stored until the tournament is deleted (see "Keeping and deleting information").
Live Skins roundsThe course, the round's settings, group names, player names and handicaps, each group's scores for each hole, and the codes that let groups join. Stored until the round is deleted.
Free trialThe email address you enter, so we can email you a 6-digit code to prove it is yours. We then keep a one-way "fingerprint" (a scrambled version that cannot be turned back into the address) of that email address and of your phone, so each person and each phone gets one free trial. Our sign-in system also keeps the address itself and the time you verified it.
Unlock codesThe code you enter, the time, and a one-way fingerprint of your phone, so a code can be used once per phone.
Emailing teams their codesIf a tournament organizer enters players' email addresses and taps "Email the teams", those addresses and the tournament details for each team are sent to our server and our email service for the moment it takes to send the emails. We do not keep the addresses. We keep only counts (how many emails went out and when) to stay within sending limits. The email says it was sent for the organizer, and replies go to the organizer if they gave a reply address.
Course searchThe words you type when searching for a golf course are sent to OpenGolfAPI (a free third-party course database) to find matching courses. Nothing else is sent to it.

About the phone "fingerprint"

To give one free trial or one use of a code per phone, the app creates a scrambled (hashed) value from an identifier that stays with the phone (on Android the device's app-specific ID; on iPhone a random ID kept in the secure Keychain). Only the scrambled value is sent. It is not used for advertising, and it cannot be used to find out who you are.

Who processes information for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in (the email codes) and server functions. Servers are in the United States (East US, North Virginia).
  • Brevo delivers the emails we send (trial codes and team codes).
  • OpenGolfAPI answers course searches.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store deliver the app and, if you buy it in the app, handle your payment. We do not see your payment details.

These services may process technical information such as your IP address while handling a request. We do not give anyone your information for their own marketing.

Permissions

The app uses the internet connection. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, except that when you choose to import a roster file or save the roster template, the app uses the file you pick. When you open an email draft, it opens your own mail app.

Keeping and deleting information

Tournament and live-round information is kept so that teams can rejoin and see results afterwards. To have a tournament, a live round, or an email address deleted, email tiwwp222@gmail.com with the code you used (or the tournament name and the email address) and we will delete it. If you ask us to delete your free-trial information, we may keep the one-way fingerprints so the same person or phone cannot start another free trial; they cannot be used to identify you.

Children

GolfGames is meant for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, and the database can only be reached through functions that check a code first. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your tournament and organizer codes private: anyone with a team's code can enter that team's scores.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the date at the top.
